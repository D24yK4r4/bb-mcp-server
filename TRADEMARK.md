## What you should do

If you fork bb-mcp-server and modify it, **rename the fork**. This is
standard open-source etiquette and protects both you and the upstream
project from user confusion. Common patterns:

- `<your-handle>-mcp-server`
- `<purpose>-mcp-server` (e.g. `redteam-mcp-server`)
- A wholly new name with a one-line credit ("based on bb-mcp-server by
  [project author]")

## Why this policy exists

The licences under which bb-mcp-server is distributed allow free
copying, modification, and redistribution. They do not prevent name
confusion. Without a separate trademark policy, anyone could publish
a fork called "bb-mcp-server" with different (potentially insecure)
behaviour, and users would have no reliable way to tell which is the
original. This policy makes that line clear.
